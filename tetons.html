The Grand Teton National Park is a national park in northwestern Wyoming known for its dramatic mountain peaks, alpine lakes, and spectacular views. It is home to the Teton Range, which features some of the most iconic and challenging alpine climbs in the United States. The park is also known for its abundant wildlife, including moose, elk, bears, and bison.
