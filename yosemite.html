Yosemite

Yosemite National Park is a national park in central California known for its massive granite cliffs, towering waterfalls, and spectacular mountain scenery. It is home to famous landmarks like El Capitan and Half Dome, which attract climbers and hikers from around the world. The park is also known for its giant sequoia trees, diverse wildlife, and breathtaking valleys.
